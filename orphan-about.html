Our Mission & Vision

At Abuhuraira Mosque, we believe every orphan deserves to feel special and well-cared for. Our mission is to provide tailored clothing that preserves their dignity and brings joy during Eid, special events, and winter seasons.

We work closely with local tailors and use precise measurements collected through this system to ensure a perfect fit. We envision a future where no orphan in our community lacks proper clothing.

Privacy & Data Ethics

All personal and measurement data is stored securely on a database, respecting the privacy of each orphan. The information is only accessible to authorized mosque staff and is never shared without consent. We use industry-standard practices to ensure confidentiality.
